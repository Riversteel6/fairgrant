Reviewer checklist

  • Confirm unit, eligibility, weights and caps with the participants.
  • Confirm input order was fixed before seeing tie outcomes.
  • Review budget sensitivity before changing an approved budget.
  • Import CSV amount columns as TEXT: spreadsheets may silently round integers longer than 15 digits. This HTML and audit.json preserve exact decimal values. Exports contain labels only, not payment addresses.

Hamilton rounding is not budget-monotone. This calculator is not for cumulative vesting, splitting large recipient groups, or automated payouts. Caps marked binding refer to the ideal allocation stage, not an award that merely rounds to its cap.
